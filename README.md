## A smooth experience for customers

- **Simple ordering** — browse, add to cart and order in a few taps; delivery charges are shown up front for Sylhet, Dhaka and the rest of Bangladesh.
- **Flexible payment** — bKash, Nagad, bank transfer or cash on delivery.
- **Order tracking** — customers follow their order and courier status using the order number and phone.
- **Instant invoices** — professional PDF invoices for every order and booking.
- **Service booking** — each service has its own booking form, pricing options and confirmation with a reference number.
- **Trust & support** — customer reviews, WhatsApp chat, an AI assistant that answers questions day and night, and clear legal pages.

## A powerful control panel for the owner

Everything on the website is managed from one secure admin panel — **no developer needed for daily work**.

- **Sales & orders** — orders, bookings, customers, invoices, coupons, flash sales, courier booking and refunds.
- **Content** — products, services, homepage sections, banners, blog, FAQs, legal pages, announcements and media library.
- **Reports** — sales, revenue and payment reports with one-click **PDF**, **Excel** and **CSV** downloads. Reports carry your logo, business details and a QR code, and the details always follow what you set in Settings.
- **AI helpers** — write descriptions, fill forms, improve text and answer customers, using the AI services you connect.
- **Marketing & visitors** — Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, visitor statistics and email newsletters.
- **System Health** — one page showing whether every connected service is working, with a green tick when fine and the exact reason and fix steps when not.
- **Backup & safety** — automatic daily backups to your own cloud drive, two-step login, fingerprint/passkey sign-in, activity history and role-based access for team members.

## Built for reliability

- Fast on mobile data and installable like an app.
- Secure by design: protected admin area, encrypted secrets and activity logging.
- Daily automatic backups with verification, plus a tested recovery plan.
- Runs on trusted cloud services with a custom domain, and costs very little to operate.
